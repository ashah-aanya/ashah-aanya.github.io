Studio

“Through multiple self-portraits, I investigate how identity contains paradoxes: appearing unified yet composed of disparate voices, stable yet constantly evolving. I explore these contradictions, observing how my sense of self shifts even as it strives for coherence.”

— from my artist’s statement

Nine years in oil, with printmaking, watercolor, and charcoal alongside it. I translate emotion — often from my own poetry — into color, apply the paint with my hands, and move between thin washes that let a figure dissolve into its background and thick alla prima strokes that assert it.

Selected work

From my AP 2-D Art & Design portfolio (AP Score: 5) — a sustained investigation into self-portraiture and duality.

The Turing Test, in oil

Human or AI

Credo.ai · Agents of Trust Summit · 2025

An exhibit where I hung my oil self-portraits beside their AI-generated replicas and asked 300+ AI-ethics leaders, policymakers, and executives to tell them apart — then argued with me about authorship, creativity, and what we owe to responsible AI. The work is now permanently displayed in Credo.ai’s offices.

Medium & process
Oil painting 9 yrs Oil on canvas, glazing and alla prima with linseed oil or slow-dry medium.
Printmaking 2 yrs Monotype prints with oil-based inks on an etching press.
Drawing & watercolor 11 yrs Willow charcoal and colored pencil layered over watercolor inks.
Acrylic painting 11 yrs Underpainting beneath the oils, and standalone works.
Digital tools 6 yrs Procreate for developing and collaging reference compositions.
