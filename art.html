The Turing Test, in oil

Credo.AI · Agents of Trust Summit

An invite-only exhibit at Credo.AI’s Agents of Trust Summit where 100+ executives, policymakers, and AI-governance leaders tried to tell my oil paintings apart from DALL·E replicas — then argued with me about authorship, creativity, and what we owe to responsible AI. It’s now permanently displayed at Credo.AI’s San Francisco offices.
